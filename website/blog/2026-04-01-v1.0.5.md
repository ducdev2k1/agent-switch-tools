**v1.0.5** adds system autostart and advanced webhook capabilities.

{/* truncate */}

## What's New

### In-App Updates

- Update notification dialog with "Install & Restart" action
- Manual "Check now" button in General settings
- Clear distinction between "Update available" vs "You're on latest"

### System Autostart

- Launch silently into system tray on login
- Guarantees continuous background tracking

### Advanced Webhook

- Member email field for user identification
- Option to include OAuth credentials in webhook payload (with security warning)

---

_v1.0.5 focuses on system-level lifecycle management (autostart & auto-update) while expanding webhook capabilities for enterprise endpoints._
